CAREER SUPPORT & PLACEMENT POLICY:
The Department of Training & Placement organizes technical training, mock interviews, soft skills development, competitive coding workshops, and resume clinics. Under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), mandatory industry immersion and internships are built into curricula. Placement drives follow company-specific eligibility thresholds, and participation is open to registered eligible students.

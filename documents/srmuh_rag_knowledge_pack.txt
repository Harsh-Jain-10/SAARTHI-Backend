1. RAG ANSWER RULES & OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Answer with the programme, academic year, applicant category, and fee component made explicit. Never call annual tuition the complete cost; mention that one-time, annual, examination, hostel, and transport charges may be separate.
- For deadlines, current availability, admissions, eligibility, scholarship continuation, transport routes, and hostel vacancies, retrieve the linked live official page at answer time. If unavailable, state that the date or amount needs confirmation.
- Never promise admission, a scholarship, an internship, a placement, a package, or a specific job. State whether a figure is a university-published highlight, historical example, or scheme condition.
- Keep the academic year attached to every fee. This pack includes 2027-28 annual tuition in the catalogue, and separately labels additional fee items from a 2026-27 official fee page. Do not add them into one total as if they were the same cycle.
- When official sources disagree, disclose the disagreement and cite both. Do not silently choose a more favorable figure.
- For questions beyond the documented facts, do not infer. Offer the official office/contact or source page listed here. Avoid giving legal, visa, or medical advice; route to the responsible office or authority.
- Recommended concise style: Lead with the direct answer, then add one relevant qualification and an official source. Example: "The admissions portal lists B.Tech CSE tuition at INR 3,00,000 per year for 2027-28. That is tuition only; other charges may apply. Please confirm the live schedule on the official portal."
Source: Current admissions portal https://admissions.srmuniversity.ac.in/

5. ADMISSIONS, ELIGIBILITY, AND SCHOLARSHIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Application Process: Apply through the official admissions portal at https://apply.srmuniversity.ac.in/admission-procedure or https://admissions.srmuniversity.ac.in/
- Eligibility: Requirements vary by programme. For example, B.Tech CSE requires 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics compulsory plus a third subject and minimum qualifying marks.
- SRMUH Scholar Search Programme:
  * Offers tuition fee scholarships up to 100%, subject to scheme criteria, seat availability, and first-come-first-served policy.
  * SRMHCAT Score Bands:
    - Score 95 or above: 100% tuition scholarship
    - Score 92 to 94: 75% tuition scholarship
    - Score 90 to 91: 50% tuition scholarship
    - Score 88 to 89: 25% tuition scholarship
    - Score 85 to 87: 10% tuition scholarship
  * CUET Best-Five Score Bands (limited to first 500 applicants):
    - 95% or above: 100% tuition scholarship
    - 90% to 94.99%: 75% tuition scholarship
    - 85% to 89.99%: 50% tuition scholarship
    - 80% to 84.99%: 25% tuition scholarship
    - 70% to 79.99%: 10% tuition scholarship
  * Other Categories: Chancellor's scholarship, defence personnel, Haryana domicile, sports quota, and alumni scholarships.
  * Continuation: Subject to maintaining minimum CGPA and no-backlog conditions.
  Source: https://apply.srmuniversity.ac.in/student-scholarships

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ PATTERNS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Where is SRMUH located?
A: SRM University Delhi-NCR is located at 39, Rajiv Gandhi Education City, Sonepat, Haryana 131029.

Q: What is the fee for B.Tech CSE?
A: The 2027-28 admissions catalogue lists B.Tech CSE annual tuition at INR 3,00,000 per year. Additional charges (registration, security deposit, examination, hostel/transport) are separate.

Q: Is there a 100% placement guarantee?
A: No. Placement eligibility and selection depend on company-set criteria. The university provides extensive career support and training, but does not guarantee a specific job or salary.

Q: What is the highest placement package?
A: The university-published placement highlight is 50 LPA, with over 925+ companies visiting and 2,024+ job offers.

Q: Can I get a 100% scholarship?
A: Yes, under the SRMUH Scholar Search Programme, applicants scoring 95 or above in SRMHCAT or 95%+ in CUET (top 500) are eligible for a 100% tuition scholarship, subject to availability and continuation terms.

Q: How much does the hostel cost?
A: In-campus accommodation starts at INR 1,85,000/year for boys' triple occupancy, INR 2,35,000/year for double occupancy, and INR 3,35,000/year for single occupancy. Girls' double occupancy is INR 2,35,000/year and single is INR 3,35,000/year. All rooms include meals and amenities.

Q: Why do campus acreage figures differ?
A: The university homepage mentions 47.89 acres, while the 2024-25 Annual Report states 47.38 acres. Both are university sources.

SRM UNIVERSITY DELHI-NCR, SONEPAT — ADMISSIONS, ELIGIBILITY & SCHOLARSHIP SCHEMES

ADMISSIONS PROCESS & ELIGIBILITY:
- Applications must be submitted through the university's online admission portal at https://apply.srmuniversity.ac.in/admission-procedure or https://admissions.srmuniversity.ac.in/
- General Eligibility:
  * For B.Tech Programmes: Passed 10+2 examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with one of Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subject/Computer Science/IT, securing minimum required marks.
  * For Post-Graduate Programmes: Recognized Bachelor's degree in relevant discipline with required aggregate percentage.
  * For Law Programmes: CLAT, LSAT-India, or SRMHCAT score with qualifying examination.

SRMUH SCHOLAR SEARCH PROGRAMME (SCHOLARSHIPS UP TO 100%):
The university awards generous merit scholarships on annual tuition fees.

1. SRMHCAT Examination Score Bands:
- Score 95 or above: 100% tuition fee scholarship
- Score 92 to 94: 75% tuition fee scholarship
- Score 90 to 91: 50% tuition fee scholarship
- Score 88 to 89: 25% tuition fee scholarship
- Score 85 to 87: 10% tuition fee scholarship

2. CUET Best-Five Percentage Score Bands (First 500 applicants limit):
- 95% or above: 100% tuition fee scholarship
- 90% to 94.99%: 75% tuition fee scholarship
- 85% to 89.99%: 50% tuition fee scholarship
- 80% to 84.99%: 25% tuition fee scholarship
- 70% to 79.99%: 10% tuition fee scholarship

3. Additional Special Scholarship Categories:
- Chancellor's Financial Assistance Scheme
- Wards of Serving / Retired Defence and Paramilitary Personnel
- Haryana Domicile Scholarship Scheme (as per State Government norms)
- Sports Quota Scholarships (National / State level achievers)
- SRM Alumni & Sibling Fee Concession Schemes

Scholarship Continuation Terms:
Scholarships apply to annual tuition only. Continuation into subsequent academic years requires maintaining a prescribed minimum CGPA (typically 8.0+), 75%+ attendance, and clear standing with zero backlogs or disciplinary records.

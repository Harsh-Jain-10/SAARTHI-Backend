SRM UNIVERSITY DELHI-NCR, SONEPAT — CAMPUS FACILITIES, RESEARCH & STUDENT LIFE

CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE:
- Fully air-conditioned smart classrooms with multimedia audio-visual aids.
- Central University Auditorium with seating capacity of 1,000 attendees.
- 24/7 Campus Health Centre with resident medical officers, nurses, and emergency ambulance on call.
Official Source: https://www.srmuniversity.ac.in/campus-facilities

LABORATORIES & RESEARCH CENTRES:
- Advanced computing labs, mechanical engineering machine shops, chemistry and physics practical labs.
- Centre for Drug Design Discovery and Development (C4D): Focuses on antimicrobial resistance, medicinal chemistry, infectious disease diagnostics, and drug formulation. Funded by major national agencies including DBT, ICMR, DSIR, SERB, and DST.
Official Sources: https://apply.srmuniversity.ac.in/academic-facilities and https://srmuniversity.ac.in/c4d

CENTRAL LIBRARY:
- State-of-the-art library automated with Koha Library Management System and RFID technology.
- Self-check-in and check-out kiosks, barcode scanners, and online catalog (OPAC).
- Access to digital databases, IEEE Xplore, DELNET, ScienceDirect, and Shodh Ganga.
- Borrowing Privileges:
  * Undergraduate students: 2 books for 14 days
  * Postgraduate & Research scholars: 4 books for 14 days
  * Faculty members: 5 books for 30 days
  * Staff members: 2 books for 14 days
- General Working Hours: 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM (extended during examinations).
Official Source: https://www.srmuniversity.ac.in/srmuh-library

SPORTS & PHYSICAL EDUCATION:
- Facilities for basketball, volleyball, football, cricket, lawn tennis, badminton, table tennis, chess, and carrom.
- Fully equipped gym and dedicated yoga center.
- Sports timings: 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.
Official Source: https://apply.srmuniversity.ac.in/department/physical-education-sports

STUDENT WELFARE, CLUBS & NATIONAL INITIATIVES:
- Student Welfare Department oversees grievance resolution, student counseling, cultural fests, and technical hackathons.
- Active National Cadet Corps (NCC) and National Service Scheme (NSS) units.
- NEP SAARTHI Initiative: Student ambassadors promoting awareness of National Education Policy (NEP 2020), interdisciplinary learning, and industry skill-sets.
Official Sources:

- https://srmuniversity.ac.in/national-cadet-corps-ncc
- https://srmuniversity.ac.in/national-service-scheme-nss

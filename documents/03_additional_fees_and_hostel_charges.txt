HOSTEL CHARGES & IN-CAMPUS ACCOMMODATION:
The university provides separate air-cooled and air-conditioned hostels for boys and girls on campus.

Boys' In-Campus Hostel Rates:
- Triple Occupancy: INR 1,85,000 per year
- Double Occupancy: INR 2,35,000 per year
- Single Occupancy: INR 3,35,000 per year

Girls' In-Campus Hostel Rates:
- Double Occupancy: INR 2,35,000 per year
- Single Occupancy: INR 3,35,000 per year

Hostel Amenities Included:
- Furnished rooms with bed, study table, chair, and wardrobe
- Nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals served in dining halls
- Attached washrooms with geyser facilities
- High-speed Wi-Fi internet connectivity
- 24/7 Power backup and clean drinking water (RO purified)
- 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric security, and dedicated wardens
- Medical center with resident doctor and ambulance support
- Shuttle transport connectivity to major transit hubs

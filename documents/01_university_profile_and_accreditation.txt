SRM UNIVERSITY DELHI-NCR, SONEPAT — UNIVERSITY PROFILE & ACCREDITATION

Location:
39, Rajiv Gandhi Education City, Delhi-NCR, Sonepat, Haryana 131029.

Legal Establishment:
Established in 2013 under Haryana State Legislature Act No. 26 of 2014.
Recognized under Section 2(f) of the University Grants Commission (UGC) Act, 1956.

Accreditation and Rankings:
Accredited with NAAC A+ Grade (reported in the 2024-25 Annual Report).
Approved by relevant statutory bodies including Bar Council of India (BCI) for Law and AICTE for technical programmes.

Campus Size & Discrepancy:
The official homepage advertises 47.89 acres, while the 2024-25 Annual Report and institutional compliance filings state 47.38 acres. Both are university publications.

Academic Offerings Overview:
70+ programmes offered across 6 faculties with 50+ international and industry collaborations.
